If you want to add the NVHHH receding hareline calendar to your iPhone\* (so you are always in sync with our runs), first try clicking on [this link](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/55k6rnam11akkav5vljqlsc6lo%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics) from your iPhone but if that doesn't work follow these steps:

1. Go to Settings App
2. Touch on "Mail, Contacts, Calendars"
3. Touch "Add Account..."
4. Touch "Other"
5. Touch "Add Subscribed Calendar" and then enter: `http://www.google.com/calendar/ical/55k6rnam11akkav5vljqlsc6lo@group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics`
6. Touch "Next" button in upper right hand corner and then "Save" (change description if you want)
7. Go to your Calendars App and you should see entries that pertain to NVHHH!

\* — While this is the procedure for the iPhone, other smartphones should be similar.
