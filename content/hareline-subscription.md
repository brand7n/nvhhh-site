The easiest way to keep the NVHHH receding hareline in sync with your phone's calendar app is to open the subscription link below on your phone. It will add the calendar as a *subscribed* calendar that updates automatically — not a one-time import.

[**Subscribe to the NVHHH Hareline**](webcal://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/55k6rnam11akkav5vljqlsc6lo%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics)

If tapping the link doesn't work, follow the steps for your phone:

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Calendar** → **Accounts** → **Add Account**.
3. Tap **Other** → **Add Subscribed Calendar**.
4. Enter: `https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/55k6rnam11akkav5vljqlsc6lo@group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics`
5. Tap **Next**, then **Save**.
6. Open the **Calendar** app — NVHHH events should appear.

## Android (Google Calendar app)

1. Open the **Google Calendar** app.
2. Tap the **+** (or the menu ☰) → **Add by URL**.
3. Enter: `https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/55k6rnam11akkav5vljqlsc6lo@group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics`
4. Tap **Add calendar**.
5. NVHHH events should now appear and stay in sync.

## Other calendar apps

Most calendar apps (Outlook, Samsung, etc.) support adding a calendar by URL. Use the same `https://.../basic.ics` address above.
