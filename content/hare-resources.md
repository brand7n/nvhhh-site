Here are some useful resources for laying trail.

- [Purple Lizard Maps](http://www.purplelizard.com/collections/maps) — Probably the best maps around for trails in Rothrock, Bear Meadows, Greenwood Furnace, Shingletown, Coopers Gap, etc.
- [Latitude/Longitude Locator](http://www.itouchmap.com/latlong.html) — Starting trail in the middle of nowhere and need to give people GPS coordinates to find you?
- [Latitude/Longitude Locator with Elevation data](http://veloroutes.org/) — Cycling site but valuable for us too
- [Kraut's map of Scotia (pdf)](assets/images/scotia_gamelands.pdf) circa 2005 — Best map of Scotia we have but could probably use an update.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) — Wiki style mapping site where users can upload GPS tracks and tag them to build out trail maps.

## Nittany Standard Trail Markings

| Symbol | Description |
| --- | --- |
| B or BN | Beer/Beer Near — Could also stand for beverage check. BVN - beer very near |
| X | Check — Trail goes in any of 369°; look for on-ons |
| On-On ![On-On](assets/images/on-on.png) | Lets you know you are on trail; Yell ON-ON as you pass to let those behind you where trail is going. |
| ![False](assets/images/false.png) or F | False trail/Bad trail — Go back to the last check and find another trail. |
| ---> | Arrow — Either the hare or someone from the pack is telling you which way trail goes |
| -/-/-/--> | Hare's Arrow — Always marks true trail. |

## Other Trail Markings

You may see these on trail especially if you travel to another hash.

| Symbol | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ![Back check](assets/images/bc.png) | Back check — Count back that number of on-ons and that mark becomes your new check. |
| YBF | You've been f\*cked! — Devious hares will lead you up a steep hill or down a long false and snicker as they lay a YBF |
